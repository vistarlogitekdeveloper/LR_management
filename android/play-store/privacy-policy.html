VTMS (LR Management) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

VTMS is a transport management app made by Vistar Logitek Private Limited ("Vistar", "we"). It is meant for Vistar employees and authorised partners, who use it to create and track lorry receipts (LRs), record deliveries and upload proof of delivery. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

DataWhy
Login details: your username or email, and a session token To sign you in and check what you are allowed to do. Your password is sent only to our server, over an encrypted connection, and is never shown to other users.
Name, phone number and work role, as set up by your company administrator To show who created or updated each record.
Proof of delivery (POD) photos and documents you take with the camera or choose from your phone To record that a consignment was delivered. The app only uses the camera or your files when you tap to add a POD.
Addresses and map locations you choose for pickup and delivery points To show routes and delivery points. The app does not read your phone's GPS location or track where you are.
Consignment, vehicle, driver and billing details you enter To run the transport business, which is the app's purpose.
App usage events, for example screens opened and actions taken, linked to your user ID; and crash or error details To fix problems and improve the app. We do not use them for advertising.

How it is stored and shared

  • Data is sent over HTTPS to Vistar's servers and stored there.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not show ads.
  • Records are visible to authorised users in your organisation, and to customers or partners whose consignments they concern, for example a delivery's POD.
  • Maps and address search use Google Maps and similar map services. They receive the map area and the search text, not your identity.
  • We disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Transport and billing records, including PODs, are kept as long as Indian tax and commercial law requires, and then deleted. Usage events are kept for no more than 24 months.

Your choices and deleting your account

Accounts are created by your company administrator. To see, correct or delete your personal data, or to close your account, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days. Business records that the law requires us to keep, such as invoices, are kept after an account is closed. The personal details on them are kept to the minimum.

Children

VTMS is a work app for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, if the change is significant, tell users in the app.
